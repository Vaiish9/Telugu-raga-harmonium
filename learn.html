🌸 Music Learning Basics

Start with the fundamentals and gradually build your musical understanding.

🎵

1. Swaras

Swaras are the basic musical notes used in Indian music.

Sa Ri Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni
These seven basic swaras form the foundation of melody.
🎹

2. Octaves

The same swara can occur at different pitch levels.

Lower • Sa
Middle Sa
Upper Sa •
Practice all three ranges on the virtual harmonium.
🎼

3. Ragas

A raga is a musical framework built from selected swaras and characteristic movements.

Raga = Musical framework
Explore different ragas on the Ragas page.
⬆️

4. Arohanam

Arohanam describes the ascending movement of swaras in a raga.

Sa → Ri → Ga → Ma → Pa
Think of it as moving upward through the swara sequence.
⬇️

5. Avarohanam

Avarohanam describes the descending movement of swaras in a raga.

Pa → Ma → Ga → Ri → Sa
Practice descending sequences slowly on the harmonium.
🥁

6. Talam

Talam provides rhythmic structure through repeating beats and cycles.

Rhythm • Beat • Cycle
Melody and rhythm work together to create musical structure.
